Why Moke Gardens

Discover a comfortable home in the scenic Lukenya area, with access to Nairobi and the amenities of Athi River.

Illustrative visual

Affordability

Through the Government of Kenya’s Affordable Housing Projects initiative, Moke Gardens offers quality apartment homes at competitive rates in the Mavoko area.

Accessibility

Enjoy access to Nairobi and nearby social amenities while coming home to a quieter setting. Athi River is served by rail connections and is close to Daystar University, Lukenya Getaway, Pine Breeze Getaway and Small World Country Club.

Nature

The development is designed to complement the serene surroundings, with spacious windows connecting interiors to the outdoors and supporting natural ventilation.

Our Story

Lukenya is an emerging area for home buyers and investors. As infrastructure develops and the area connects with Nairobi’s economy, Moke Gardens was created to bring homes to this growing neighbourhood while preserving a peaceful setting. The gated-community concept reflects a commitment to security and residents’ comfort.

Our Vision

To create a world-class estate that changes the way people live, work and play through innovative, eco-friendly and affordable developments.

Our Mission

To create quality, affordable housing through a goal-oriented team that delivers innovative ideas, sustainable wealth and a signature brand.
